Salaryman Iseka


About the game

Imagine being an unsuspecting salaryman just minding your own business, then suddenly getting transported to another world with a bunch of high school students. With no blessings from the gods to speak of, you are cast out of the castle with a small pouch of gold for your inconvenience. Find a way to survive (and get home?) in this unknown world where you don't even know how much things cost, much less the oral and written language, with only your skill as a salaryman. It is the player's job to understand to cost of things by understanding the environment and getting clues from the people of the town.


My role and contributions

I enjoyed creating the concept of this game with my friends very much and I plan to continue working on this as my personal project. I handle the NPC AI where I made sure to make it look like the NPCs had their own schedule to keep; towns people visiting shops, talking with people, shopkeepers tending to their store, people training with the bow or sword, and, of course, NPCs visiting the players shop to buy (or judge the price) of their items for sale. Additionally, I also set up each scriptable object for each item available in the game, including the item information and acceptable range of costs the player can sell them. Go too high or too low, the NPCs will be unwilling to buy them.






Death's Brew


About the game

A memorization game where you play as a skeleton tavern owner who serves drinks to adventurers, but there is more to it than that. On the side, you are actually an assassin for hire! Behind the counter, you will be notified of your targets and you must remember what kind of drink to serve to get paid.


My role and contributions

I was responsible for managing the targets and ensuring that all NPCs looked unique, from the color of their heads to their toes. To achieve this, I applied mathematical methods to randomize NPC appearances. I used Blender to separate each character's colored part to keep their uniqueness from their other parts. I also created the logic that randomly selects which NPC is designated as the target and simultaneously displays their appearance behind the counter for the player to see.
